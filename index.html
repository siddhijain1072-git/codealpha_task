What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

Attackers may try to steal:

  • 🔐 Passwords
  • 🔢 OTPs
  • 💳 Banking information
  • 👤 Personal information

🚨 How to Recognize Phishing

📧 Suspicious Sender

Check the sender's email address carefully.

⚠️ Urgent Message

Be careful with messages saying "Act Now!"

🔗 Suspicious Link

Check the website URL before clicking.

🔢 OTP Request

Never share your OTP or password.

📧 Example of a Phishing Email

From: security@bank-example.com

Subject: URGENT! Account Verification Required

Your account will be blocked today. Click the link below immediately to verify your account.

🎭 Social Engineering

Social engineering is a technique where attackers manipulate people into performing actions that benefit the attacker.


😨 Fear

"Your account will be blocked."

⏰ Urgency

"Respond immediately."

🎁 Reward

"You have won a prize!"

👤 Impersonation

Pretending to be a trusted person.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe?

✅ Think before clicking.

✅ Check the sender.

✅ Verify website URLs.

✅ Never share OTP or passwords.

✅ Use multi-factor authentication.

✅ Report suspicious messages.
